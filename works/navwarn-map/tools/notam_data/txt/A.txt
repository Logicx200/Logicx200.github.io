2.SPECIAL PROCEDURE FOR EMERGENCY DESCENT
2.1 IN AN EMERGENCY SITUATION THAT THREATENS FLIGHT SAFETY, THE PIC(PILOT IN COMMAND)
HAS THE RIGHT TO CHANGE THE ORIGINALLY ASSIGNED FLIGHT LEVEL, BUT SHALL IMMEDIATELY
REPORT TO THE ATC AND WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE ACTION TAKEN.THE SPECIAL PROCEDURE FOR
EMERGENCY DESCENT SHALL BE: TURN RIGHT 30 DEGREES FROM THE AIRCRAFT'S CURRENT FLIGHT
TRACK, MAINTAIN THE HEADING TO ESTABLISH A 5NM (9.3KM) OFFSET, THEN DESCEND TO THE NEW
FLIGHT LEVEL AND TURN BACK TO THE ORIGINAL ROUTE BY ATC.
2.2 IF THE AIRCRAFT IS UNABLE TO COMPLY WITH THE ABOVE REQUIREMENTS, THE PIC SHALL
ESTABLISH CONTACT WITH ATC AS SOON AS POSSIBLE AND STATE THE INTENTION.
2.3 ONCE THE SAFETY THREAT HAS BEEN RESOLVED, THE PIC SHALL ADVISE ATC PROMPTLY IF
REQUIRING RADAR VECTORS TO REJOIN THE ORIGINAL ROUTE. ATC SHALL CONTINUE TO PROVIDE AIR
TRAFFIC SERVICES CONSIDERING CURRENT TRAFFIC FLOW, AIRSPACE STRUCTURE, METEOROLOGICAL
CONDITIONS AND OTHER AIRSPACE USER ACTIVITIES.
